Reanalysis vs airport station

Hourly reanalysis smooths wind over a 9–50 km cell and misses many gusty hours. Calibration maps its speeds, month by month, onto the distribution measured at the airport (quantile mapping trained on the same hours at the station), and keeps the reanalysis timing and direction for this point.
